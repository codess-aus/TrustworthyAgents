# Chapter 1 · Hook + Context

## Slide 1 · Title

The opening frames the urgency: many teams were asked to secure AI features and then, almost immediately, secure full AI agents. The talk promise is practical: leave with a clear way to build and deploy agents responsibly.

## Slide 2 · The Vibe Shift

- **2024:** Add AI to your app.
- **2026:** Your app *is* the agent.

The key reassurance is that security fundamentals still matter and are even more valuable in agentic systems.

## Slide 3 · What We’re Covering

1. Build and deploy safe, effective AI agents.
2. Understand key security concerns in development.
3. Maintain user and data privacy.
